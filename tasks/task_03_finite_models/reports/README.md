# Reports

The final report must select exactly one legal outcome from `../task.yaml` and justify it without
overclaiming.

At minimum it must include:

- the locked problem and assumption boundary;
- search coverage, configurations, and reproducibility instructions;
- all material conjectures, counterexamples, and revisions;
- certificate and verifier results;
- the status of the metatheory gate;
- unresolved obligations and known limitations;
- an explicit explanation of why the selected outcome is the strongest justified outcome.

For `restricted-case-proved`, the changed statement and complete assumption delta are mandatory.
For `bounded-frontier-certified`, the exact exhaustive bounds are mandatory. For
`inconclusive-but-audited`, failed approaches and open questions are first-class deliverables.
